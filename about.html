I've spent 13 years in marketing and communications, from university outreach to retail to a two brand marketing role balancing a two location dental practice and a construction consulting firm at the same time.

Along the way I learned what separates strategic marketing that makes a difference from reaction marketing that just keeps people paid and looking busy. The difference is having a clear goal, a real plan, and the discipline to stick with it long enough to see what your numbers are telling you.

I started Red District Creative to work with business owners who need that kind of partner: someone who will support their goals, communicate all available options, and do the work well.
