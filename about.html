I've spent 13 years in marketing and communications, from university outreach to retail to a two brand marketing role juggling a dental practice and a construction consulting firm at the same time.

Along the way I learned what separates marketing that works from marketing that just keeps people busy. The difference is almost never talent or budget. It's having a clear goal, a real plan, and the discipline to stick with it long enough to see what the numbers say.

I started Red District Creative to work with business owners who want that kind of partner: someone who will respect their goals, tell them the truth, and do the work well.

Values

The lessons I've learned along the way, and the promises I make to every client.

  1. Your business, your decision.

    I will always show you every option, not just the one I prefer. I'll give you my honest advice, and then the decision is yours, even when it isn't the one I'd make. It's your business. I'm here to support it.

  2. Experts in our crafts.

    You know your business: how to make it, build it and run it. I know how the internet works and how to get people to see you on their phones, TVs and computers. Let me do my part. Trust me, and give it time. Real results don't happen overnight, but they do happen.

    A lesson from Gene Steiner.
