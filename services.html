Where your customers find you, in order of importance

Most small businesses start with Facebook and never get to the foundation. I work in the opposite order, because each layer depends on the one before it.

  1. Your websiteThe one place you own. Every search, ad, post and email should lead here, and it has to load fast, answer questions and make booking or buying easy.
  2. GoogleSearch results, Maps, your Business Profile, reviews and AI answers. This is where people go when they're ready to buy, and Google relies on your website to decide whether to show you.
  3. EmailBrings customers back for free. Your list is an audience no algorithm can take away.
  4. Social mediaBuilds familiarity and trust, when it has a plan, a goal and real content behind it. Without those, it's noise.
